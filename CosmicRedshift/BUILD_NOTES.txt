THE ANIMATION
-------------
animation/ is your CosmicRedshift package copied in BYTE-FOR-BYTE — index.html,
scripts/voiceover_script.js and audio/voiceover.mp3 are untouched, rev J label
intact. slide3.html embeds it with:

    <iframe src="animation/index.html" style="width:100%; height:1020px; ...">

It sits in its own folder so its relative paths (scripts/, audio/) do not
collide with the quiz's own scripts/ and audio/ folders. If the frame shows a
scrollbar or dead space on your display, adjust that height value only.
