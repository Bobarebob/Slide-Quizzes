What You’ve Learned:
  • Cosmological redshift comes from the expansion of space itself, not from the source moving through space — it is not a Doppler shift
  • Observed wavelength = emitted wavelength × (1 + z), so z = 1 doubles the wavelength and z = 10.3 stretches it 11.3 times
  • Our 450 nm pulse left a B-type blue star at z = 10.3, about 450 million years after the Big Bang
  • It stayed visible for 6.3 billion light-years, crossing into the infrared only at z ≈ 0.67, then arrived at Earth at just over 5 microns after 13.3 billion years
  • Distance in Gly and travel time in billions of years are the same number for light
  • The source star ran through blue main-sequence → red supergiant → supernova → black hole while its own light was still in transit
  • Olbers’ Paradox resolves through the finite age of the Universe and through redshift — the sky is full of light we cannot see, some of it from objects that no longer exist
