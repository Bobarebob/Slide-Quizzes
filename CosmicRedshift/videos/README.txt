COSMIC REDSHIFT — AVATAR VIDEOS
===============================

Two files are needed here. The filenames are referenced by the <video> tag
on intro.html and outro.html and must match exactly:

    redshift_intro.mp4    <- narrate scripts/intro_avatar_script.txt
    redshift_outro.mp4    <- narrate scripts/outro_avatar_script.txt

Both players are fixed at 4:3 aspect ratio, matching the Atmosphere module.
Render at 4:3 or the video will letterbox inside the frame.
