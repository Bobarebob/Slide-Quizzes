LAB 3A DIFFRACTION - AUDIO RECORDING LIST
Save each recording as an MP3 with exactly this file name, in this audio folder.
Until a file is here, the lab reads that line with the browser voice instead.

intro.mp3
   When: Lab page opens (fresh start)
   Line: Welcome to Lab 3A, Diffraction. Select a laser color to begin.

laser_red.mp3
   When: Red laser chosen
   Line: The red laser has been selected. Click the laser body to turn it on.

laser_green.mp3
   When: Green laser chosen
   Line: The green laser has been selected. Click the laser body to turn it on.

laser_blue.mp3
   When: Blue laser chosen
   Line: The blue laser has been selected. Click the laser body to turn it on.

on_ceiling.mp3
   When: Laser on, upper dot hits the ceiling
   Line: The upper dot is on the ceiling, which is not valid. Use the Table, Rack, and Holder controls to move the apparatus closer to the wall.

on_wall.mp3
   When: Laser on, both dots on the wall
   Line: Both dots are on the wall. Click Next when you are satisfied with their positions.

measure_L.mp3
   When: Next clicked after positioning
   Line: Measure L, the horizontal distance from the grating to the wall. Click the grating holder and drag to the right until you reach the central dot on the wall.

measure_D.mp3
   When: L entered in the data table
   Line: L is recorded in your data table. Now measure D. Click the central dot on the wall and drag upward to the upper diffraction dot.

complete_red.mp3
   When: D entered for the red laser
   Line: D is recorded. The red laser data is complete.

complete_green.mp3
   When: D entered for the green laser
   Line: D is recorded. The green laser data is complete.

complete_blue.mp3
   When: D entered for the blue laser
   Line: D is recorded. The blue laser data is complete.

switch_next.mp3
   When: Plays right after complete_*, while lasers remain
   Line: Click Next to switch to another laser.

select_next.mp3
   When: Laser chooser reappears
   Line: Now select your next laser.

all_done.mp3
   When: Plays right after complete_*, when all three lasers are done
   Line: All measurements are complete. Click Next to proceed to calculations.

calc_intro.mp3
   When: Calculation table opens
   Line: Calculations are now open. Click a Calculate button to begin each calculation.

reset.mp3
   When: Reset button or Escape key
   Line: Lab reset. Select a laser color to begin.
